New in 0.3.0
- Escalating alarm: if the sound goes on and nobody answers the alert, an alarm rings, louder and louder, also in silent mode.
- "Not the baby": one tap pauses the alerts for 10 minutes.
- Room status: "Awake for 4 min" or "Quiet for 1 h" on each station.
- Night log: your child's wake-ups, kept only on your device for 30 days.
- Nestwatch now speaks 11 languages.
