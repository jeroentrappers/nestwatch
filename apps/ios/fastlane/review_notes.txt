Nestwatch is a baby monitor that works between the user's own devices: one device is the station (in the child's room), another is the listener. There is no account, so no demo login is needed.

To test with a single iPhone:
1. On a computer, open https://nestwatch.appmire.be/app/ in Chrome, Safari or Firefox and choose "This device watches". Allow the microphone, and when asked, make the station reachable through the relay.
2. Tap "Pair a listener" in the browser. It shows a QR code.
3. On the iPhone, choose "This device listens", tap "Add a station" and scan the QR code (or tap "Use a link" and paste the invitation link shown under the code).
4. Confirm that both screens show the same number. The station tile then shows the room's sound level; speak near the computer to hear it on the iPhone. Hold "Hold to talk" to talk back, and tap "Camera" to open the station's camera.

With two iOS devices, choose "This device watches" on one and follow the same steps from step 2.

Background audio: a listener keeps playing the room's sound with the screen locked, which is the core function of a baby monitor. The station uses the microphone and, only on request from a paired listener, the camera. Time-sensitive notifications alert the parent when a quiet room starts to sound or a station is unreachable.

Encryption: connections use the Noise Protocol Framework (ChaCha20-Poly1305) for end-to-end encryption between the user's devices. Nestwatch is open source (https://github.com/jeroentrappers/nestwatch).
